// Technical Paradigm

Quantum Computing vs Classical Security

Quantum Supremacy

Qubits leverage superposition and quantum entanglement to evaluate parallel states, speeding up prime factor computations exponentially.

The RSA Vulnerability

Shor's Algorithm solves prime factorization in polynomial time, rendering current asymmetric encryption schemes vulnerable.

Lattice-Based PQC

Relies on mathematical problems intractable for both quantum and classical machines, such as Learning With Errors (LWE).
